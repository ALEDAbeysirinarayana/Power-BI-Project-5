## Workforce IQ: Financial Portfolio Analytics

## Project Overview
This Power BI project provides a sophisticated investment and portfolio management solution designed to track financial assets and risk metrics. The dashboard, titled "Work force IQ," offers deep visibility into market values, asset distribution, and transaction history to support data-driven investment strategies.

## Objectives
Monitor total portfolio health through real-time tracking of Market Value and Total Investment.
Analyze risk and performance metrics, including Max Drawdown and Unrealized PnL.
Provide a detailed breakdown of asset allocation across different classes such as Bonds, Crypto, Equity, and ETFs.
Facilitate transaction auditing by tracking historical trades, total fees, and turnover periods.

## Tools & Technologies
Microsoft Power BI: Primary platform for report development and data visualization.
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Used to calculate complex financial measures like "Annualized Return," "Daily Volatility," and "VaR 95 Parametric".
Power Query: Utilized for data modeling across multiple tables including Assets, DailyReturns, Prices, and Transactions.

## Key Features
Executive Summary: A high-level view of critical KPIs like $348.55K Total Portfolio Value and $11.22M Total Investment.
Risk & Analytics: Dedicated section for drawdown trends and historical VaR (Value at Risk) assessments.
Holdings Management: Interactive tables showing shares held, latest prices, and unrealized profit/loss by specific asset ticker.
Transaction & Cashflow: Detailed ledger tracking of signed quantities, price per unit, and market value over time.
Dynamic Filtering: Multi-select slicers for Asset Class, Ticker, and Date range for customized analysis.
